ZapFast 0.21.0 is a new look for the Mac: calmer colours, flat surfaces, rounder corners, and a new icon. It is the first release from this fork and ships for macOS only.

**Download ZapFast:** [Mac](https://github.com/Sowyu/whysapp/releases/download/v0.21.0/zapfast-v0.21.0-macos-universal.dmg)

This build is signed ad hoc and not notarized, so macOS warns the first time you open it. Right-click ZapFast in Applications, choose Open, then Open again.

![ZapFast in dark mode](https://github.com/Sowyu/whysapp/releases/download/v0.21.0/chat-dark.png)

![ZapFast in light mode](https://github.com/Sowyu/whysapp/releases/download/v0.21.0/chat-light.png)

## New

- **A new app icon:** a solid white question mark on a dark Liquid Glass tile. On macOS 26 it takes the system's glass, dark and clear looks; older versions get a flat copy.
- **A quieter look.** Neutral greys with a teal accent replace WhatsApp green. The chat list and the icon bar are one flat column, and the conversation sits in a rounded card beside them.
- **Rounder, consistent corners:** 6 points for fields and buttons, 8 for bubbles and list rows, 12 for the conversation card, dialogs and the message box.
- **No more gradients or glows.** The shadows under headers and bubbles, the lit edges and the bubble tails are gone; thin lines separate things instead.
- **The doodle wallpaper is off by default.** Turn it back on in Settings.

![The new icon](https://github.com/Sowyu/whysapp/releases/download/v0.21.0/icon.png)

## Fixed

- **The traffic lights no longer overlap the conversation** when the chat list is folded down to icons. They get a strip of their own above the card.

## Known limitations

- Only the Mac app ships. The Linux and Windows packages are gone.
- Updates come from this fork's releases, and the app accepts only releases signed with ZapFast's update key, which this fork does not have. Install new versions from this page by hand.
- Calls are still a work in progress, as in [ZapFast 0.20.0](https://github.com/crmne/zapfast/releases/tag/v0.20.0).
